Whenever I use Higgsfield, OpenMontage, or any paid AI video/image/audio generation: (1) Use cheap models only (Kling, budget models, low resolution, short duration) for anything automated, drafts, tests or iteration. Use expensive models (Veo, Seedance) only for final renders of shots that are already approved and really tight. Always show model, resolution, duration and credit cost before any paid call, never retry failed paid generations in a loop.
